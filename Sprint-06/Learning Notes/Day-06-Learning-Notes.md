# Day 6 – Apex Triggers and Event-Driven Automation

## Engineering Principle

Great software does not wait to be instructed.
It knows exactly when to act.

Enterprise software responds automatically when important business
events occur.

---

## Why Automation Matters

As the number of students, companies and applications grows, manual
follow-up becomes difficult.

Automation reduces:

- Human error
- Missed activities
- Repetitive work
- Administrative effort

Automation also ensures that business processes are followed
consistently.

---

## What is Event-Driven Software?

Event-driven software responds automatically when important business
events occur.

Examples include:

- A new Student is registered.
- A Company publishes a Job.
- A Student submits an Application.
- A Recruiter updates an interview result.
- A Placement Offer is accepted.

The important idea is:

```text
Business Event
      ↓
Automatic Response
