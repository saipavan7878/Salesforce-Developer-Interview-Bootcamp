# Day 5 Challenges

## 1. Understanding SOQL as a Business Query

Initially, SOQL can look like database syntax to memorize.

### Learning

I learned to first identify the business question and the information
required before writing the query.

## 2. Understanding Create vs Update

It was important to distinguish between creating new information and
modifying existing information.

### Learning

New business information requires a new record, while changes to
existing information require an update.

## 3. Combining SOQL and DML

The main challenge was understanding how retrieval and data modification
work together in one business transaction.

### Learning

The application should retrieve and validate information first and
perform DML only after the business rules are satisfied.
