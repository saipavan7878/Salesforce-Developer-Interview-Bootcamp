# Part 1 – Information Requirements

## Engineering Principle

Software cannot make good decisions without first finding the right
information.

## Business Question

Allow a student to apply for Microsoft.

Before writing SOQL, identify the information required by the business
decision.

## Possible Information Required

- Student CGPA
- Student Branch
- Active Backlogs
- Graduation Year
- Job Eligibility
- Application Deadline
- Existing Applications
- Number of Current Offers

## Key Learning

Before writing a SOQL query, first identify what information is required.

Every query should answer a business question.

A useful starting point is:

"I am writing this query because I need to know..."
