# Reflection – Sprint 01

Sprint 01 helped me understand the basic architecture of a Salesforce Placement Management System.

I learned how custom objects, fields and Lookup relationships are used to model real-world business data.

I also learned how SOQL retrieves information from Salesforce and how Apex can use that information to make business decisions.

The biggest learning was separating trigger logic into a handler class instead of putting all the logic directly inside the trigger.

I successfully implemented and tested CGPA eligibility validation, closing-date validation, automatic application date and application status.

I still need more practice with Apex testing, debugging and writing more advanced SOQL queries.
